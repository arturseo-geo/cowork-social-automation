---
description: Review and fill the social content calendar. Shows queued posts, identifies gaps, and generates new content sets to fill them from pending sources.
---

Use the content-calendar skill.

Arguments: $ARGUMENTS

If "fill" is provided, generate posts to fill all calendar gaps.
If "preview" or no argument, show the current queue and identify gaps without generating.
Read from and write back to the Google Sheets CMS if connected.
